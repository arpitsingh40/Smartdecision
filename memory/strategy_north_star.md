# SmartDecigen — Strategy North Star (agreed with founder, July 2026)

> This document is the strategic source of truth. Every future feature/pricing/GTM decision
> should be checked against it. It supersedes earlier "credit pack" retail thinking.

## The benchmark (what we compete on)
- NOT "better answers than ChatGPT". Answer quality converges (everyone rides the same models).
- We compete on a BETTER DECISION PROCESS whose quality COMPOUNDS with use:
  company memory + structured reasoning + evidence + execution tracking + outcome learning.
- Real competition: doing nothing, expensive consultants, gut intuition, tool-switching chaos.
- Target user sentence: "I use ChatGPT for many things, but I use SmartDecigen before
  important business decisions."

## Positioning
**The AI Chief of Staff for founders.**
- Comparison class = strategy consultant (Rs50K–5L/engagement) or Chief-of-Staff hire
  (Rs1–2L/month) — NOT ChatGPT (free). Positioning selects the comparison; the comparison
  justifies the price.
- Marketing line: "ChatGPT answers your questions. SmartDecigen holds you to what you decided."

## Mission (4 stages — Learn added to founder's 3)
1. **Understand** — find the real problem (not the surface symptom).
2. **Decide** — choose the highest-confidence path with evidence (options, risks, assumptions).
3. **Execute** — turn the decision into measurable progress.
4. **Learn** — check outcomes vs predictions later (e.g. 30 days), update confidence/calibration.
   Learn is the stage ChatGPT can never have (requires living with consequences). It makes
   the other three compound.

## The feature rule (apply to EVERY backlog item)
Build only if it improves one of:
1. Understanding the real problem.
2. Quality of the decision.
3. Probability of successful execution.
If no -> don't build it yet.

## The real product
**Reduce uncertainty in important business decisions.** Not AI, not dashboards, not OKRs.

## The investor question, made measurable
Fuzzy: "After 6 months are founders making noticeably better decisions than ChatGPT-only founders?"
Operationalized: every significant decision carries {predicted outcome + confidence, review date,
actual outcome, estimated Rs impact}. After 6 months each customer has a DECISION LEDGER with a
hit-rate and a rupee tally. The product prints the answer itself. This ledger is simultaneously:
the proof engine, the case-study generator, the renewal argument, and the switching-cost moat.

## Product roadmap — the 3 missing "organs" (build order agreed)
Today's product already has the Understand/Decide/Execute skeleton (journey clarity chat ->
direction + success_probability -> milestones; decision brain; commit/done/follow-through; cockpit).
The gap to a credible Rs7,999 Chief of Staff:

1. **Organ 1 — Decision Record + outcome loop (BUILD FIRST, mostly free, no new keys)**
   Every significant decision -> living artifact: real problem, evidence used, options considered,
   chosen path, assumptions, risks, predicted outcome + confidence, review date. On review date:
   actual outcome + Rs impact captured, calibration updated. Extends existing decisions ledger.
   Passes the "would they miss it if it disappeared" test.
2. **Organ 2 — Company Data Room (persistent memory, not per-turn attachments)**
   Upload CRM export / P&L / pricing sheet ONCE -> informs every future decision. Unify existing
   org KB (doc_trees) + per-turn file-extraction pipeline into persistent company context.
3. **Organ 3 — Deep Research with live web (consultant-killer)**
   Policy changes, competitor moves, market pricing pulled live + cited. ENGINE HAS NO WEB ACCESS
   TODAY — do not market this before it exists. Needs a web-search integration
   (Tavily vs Perplexity playbook already fetched earlier — awaiting provider choice + key).

## Pricing (value-first, replaces credit-pack retail thinking)
- **Biggest identified mistake to avoid**: pricing on tokens/credits/prompts/seats before value
  is proven. NOTE: current live product sells credit packs Rs49/399/999 with credit copy in UI —
  this is that mistake and must be re-skinned.
- **Fix**: value-priced subscription OUTSIDE, credits silently INSIDE as fair-use metering /
  COGS guard (keep the billing plumbing; hide all credit language from users; Founder OS keeps
  full token visibility).
- **Founder Plan (single plan at launch)**: list Rs7,999/month or Rs79,999/year.
  Founding-customer price Rs3,999–4,999 locked for life for first 50.
  Includes: unlimited strategic conversations (fair use), deep research, workspace memory,
  decision history, company knowledge, decision validation.
  COGS check: heavy founder on Opus ~ Rs2–4K/month tokens -> "unlimited (fair use)" is viable.
- Sell via existing LIVE Zoho as a 30-day / 365-day pass; manual renewal for first 50
  (do things that don't scale). Rs49 pack dies or becomes the trial; free trial = signup credits.
- **No per-seat pricing initially** (20 x Rs1,999 = Rs40K/month invites "why does every employee
  need this?" — no strong answer yet). Sell to the FOUNDER; one company; one workspace.
- Growth tier (Rs14,999–24,999) and Enterprise (custom) come LATER, after founding-cohort behavior
  shows whether teammates get invited / org capabilities are requested.

## GTM (proof before price)
- Stage 1: 20–50 founding customers, founders only, high touch, weekly contact.
- Channels (in order): founder case studies, referrals, LinkedIn thought leadership, live decision
  workshops, founder-community partnerships, product-led referrals. NO paid ads first.
- CAC is set by the market, not chosen. Targets: CAC payback 6–12 months, LTV:CAC >= 3:1 (5:1 healthy).
- The Decision Records ARE the case studies ("11 decisions, 8 reviewed, Rs14L estimated impact").
- 90-day question to answer with real users: "What measurable business outcome makes a founder say
  'I can't imagine running my company without this'?"
- Readiness test for premium pricing: if the product disappeared tomorrow, would the founder
  actively miss it? ("I'll just use ChatGPT" = not ready.)

## Why this matters (economics)
- Consumption pricing caps ARPU at Rs100–1,000/month with consumer churn. Decision-outcome
  positioning reaches consultant-replacement budgets (Rs5K–25K/month) with B2B retention.
  Same codebase, 10–50x revenue per user.
- Moat = the outcome ledger + company memory (models commoditize; a calibrated track record
  with THIS company does not). Every month of use raises switching cost.
- Proof-first GTM converts the scarcest resource (trust) into the cheapest channel (referrals).
